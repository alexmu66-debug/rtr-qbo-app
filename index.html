Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy applies to the RTR Accounting internal QuickBooks integration tool operated by Real to Reel.

Data Use

This application accesses QuickBooks Online account data solely for internal accounting and bookkeeping purposes by authorized Real to Reel personnel. No financial data, personal information, or QuickBooks credentials are collected, transmitted, or stored by any third party.

Data Storage

All authentication tokens and credentials are stored locally on authorized company devices only. No data is stored on external servers or shared with any third parties.

Access

Access to this tool is restricted to authorized Real to Reel accounting staff only.

For questions regarding this policy, contact Real to Reel accounting at 16118 Sherman Way, Van Nuys, CA 91406.
